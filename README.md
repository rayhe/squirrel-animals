# The Squirrel Squad

A fun, kid-friendly static site about the squirrel family: tree squirrels (gray, fox, red), chipmunks, prairie dogs, groundhogs (marmots), flying squirrels, and ground squirrels.

Built for Ray on 2026-09-15 from a sticky-note idea.

## What's here

- `index.html`: the whole site: hero, 8 animal profile cards, a tiniest-to-biggest size chart, a nutty fact machine, and a "Which Squirrel Are You Most Like?" quiz
- `style.css`: bright playful styling
- `script.js`: fact rotator and quiz logic (plain JS, no libraries)
- `images/`: 9 AI-generated cartoon illustrations (hero + one per animal), JPEG

## Viewing

Open `index.html` in any browser, or serve the folder with any static server. It is a plain static site, ready for GitHub Pages.

## Facts

Every animal fact was checked against wildlife agencies and science sources: the Tennessee Wildlife Resources Agency, the Missouri Department of Conservation, the Virginia Department of Wildlife Resources, the Alaska Department of Fish and Game, the National Wildlife Federation, and National Geographic.
